- Summary: The European Commission is developing guidelines to support the implementation of Directive 2024/1760 on corporate sustainability due diligence (CSDDD). The CSDDD targets very large EU companies, as well as non-EU companies with significant business in the EU. It aims to ensure that companies within its scope identify, prevent and bring to an end actual and potential adverse impacts on human rights and the environment in their own operations, those of their subsidiaries and in their value chains.
